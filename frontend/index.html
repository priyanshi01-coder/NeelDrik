How a scene becomes a verdict

Five stages. Only one of them is a neural network — the rest is signal processing, physics, and deliberate refusal.

  1. 01

    Domain gate

    Rules decide whether the upload is really SAR. Anything else is refused outright rather than guessed at.

  2. 02

    Preprocess

    Robust median/MAD normalisation, a Lee speckle filter, and a texture channel measured before filtering.

  3. 03

    U-Net

    A 5-class encoder–decoder labels every pixel: sea, oil, look-alike, ship, land.

  4. 04

    Decide

    Calibrated thresholds, connected components, and a wind plausibility check that can only ever weaken a call.

  5. 05

    Drift

    Lagrangian transport — current plus 3% of wind, Coriolis, RK4 — run backwards to estimate the source.

What it does

◎

Detects, and admits doubt

Oil and look-alikes genuinely overlap in SAR. Ambiguous scenes are routed to an analyst as NEEDS REVIEW rather than forced into a wrong answer.

⟳

Back-tracks the source

Deterministic physics, verified against ten closed-form solutions. A known release point is recovered to within 200 metres.

◈

Shows its working

Every verdict carries a reason trail — which threshold decided it, what the wind was, how coherent the region looked.

⬢

Exports what responders need

Slick boundaries as GeoJSON, overlays as PNG, and a full JSON record of the analysis for the case file.
